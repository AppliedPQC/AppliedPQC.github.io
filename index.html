Implementations

SageMath reference implementations

Complete, byte-exact implementations of FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA) and FIPS 206 (FN-DSA). Every numbered algorithm of each standard is implemented, and the coverage is machine-checked rather than asserted.

  • Checked against the NIST ACVP vectors, byte for byte.
  • FN-DSA follows the round-3 Falcon submission, since FIPS 206 is still unpublished, and is validated against Falcon's own KATs.
  • Teaching implementations: readable and standards-exact, but not constant-time and not for production use.
